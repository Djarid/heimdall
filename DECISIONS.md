## 1. Purpose

This log records every material decision taken across the premise PoC and the design work that followed, so the design can be checked for internal consistency and so no acceptance obligation is left without an owner. Each decision has a stable identifier, a status, the rationale, where it is realised, and its dependencies. A decision that contradicts another, or a test obligation with no owning phase, is meant to be visible here rather than discovered late.

**Status values:**

- **SETTLED** decided and realised (in code, in a document, or as a fixed design choice).
- **SPIKE-GATED** decided in principle, ratification pending a defined spike with pass criteria.
- **DEFERRED** deliberately not decided yet, with a recorded trigger that will force it.
- **OPEN** a named question that bears on the design and is not yet resolved.

**How to verify the design from this log:** every SETTLED decision should have a realisation reference; every SPIKE-GATED and DEFERRED decision should have a trigger and an owning phase; no two SETTLED decisions should conflict; every acceptance obligation in the invariants doc should trace to a decision here. Section 6 records the consistency checks.

## 6. Consistency checks

Recorded so the design can be audited against itself. Re-run these when a decision changes.

1. **No SETTLED conflict.** D20 (vocabulary in ontology, binding in control surface) and D24 (agent-scoped action-critical) are mutually consistent: agent scoping is the reason binding is not in the ontology. D22 (subject-matter not medium) and D22a (threat surface is all external content, not email) reinforce each other: both say the medium is not the type. D46 (ontology as a property-graph package) follows D25 and does not conflict with D39's directory layout: the directories hold layer intent, the package holds the loaded graph. No conflict found.
2. **Every acceptance obligation has an owner.** The four obligations in invariant 3.11 map to D25/D32 (substrate and retraction, ratified by the spike; D43 to D45 record the algorithm, mode default and caveat), D26/D27/D49 (growth and test-corpus provenance), D28 (marshalling) and D30 (flow-to-sink testing). All four are now realised and run by the Phase 2 seed and Nornir (D46, D47, D48): coverage measurement, classification correctness, reasoner soundness and flow-to-sink each have a passing harness (`ontology/tests/harness.py`). No orphan obligation.
3. **Every NOT YET TESTED invariant has a construction path, and 3.9/3.11 are now partly demonstrated.** Invariants 3.6 (action-critical half), 3.9 (coverage bound) and 3.11 trace to D20 to D30, D38 and D43 to D52. The substrate spike demonstrated the action-critical mechanism substrate-neutrally (D43 to D45); the seed and Nornir demonstrate classification, the fail-safe, reasoner soundness and agent-scoped cross-domain flow-to-sink on a labelled corpus across two domains with a principled cross-domain priority rule (D46 to D52). Invariant 3.9's coverage bound is measured (90.9 percent on the two-domain corpus). The live-store binding and coverage beyond the seed remain untested. No untested invariant lacks a build route.
4. **Every DEFERRED and OPEN item has a trigger.** D33 to D36 each record a trigger or owning phase. D25, D31, D32 and D38 are no longer spike-gated, deferred or open: the substrate spike ratified D25/D32/D38, and the second domain forced and settled D31 (with its priority principle D52). D34 (honest vs injection-induced error) remains open and is where a richer classification-correctness corpus will bite. D33, D35 and D36 remain open or deferred with their triggers. None lacks a condition that forces it.
5. **The PoC decisions carry into invariants.** D02 to D13 each map to an invariant (3.1 to 3.10). No proven PoC property is dropped. The seed preserves them: Nornir contains no model (D02, invariant 3.1), and the four PoC fields become typed nodes without gaining action-capability (D46).
6. **Threat surface is not narrowed by the staging choice, and the attach test is demonstrated.** D22a records that starting with email does not narrow the threat model to email; D29's medium attach test is satisfied (media beyond email feed the same types). The domain attach test is now demonstrated, not just structural: attaching the scheduling domain (D50) left communications, the spine, `core.py` and `unclassified.py` unchanged, confirmed by `git diff`. So the ontology cannot silently become either email-shaped or single-domain-shaped. Checked against `ONTOLOGY_CONSTRUCTION.md` sections 2.4, 4 and 4.2.
7. **Licence boundary coincides with the trust boundary.** D39 and D40: the loaded ontology is BFO-derived and Heimdall-authored (CC BY 4.0 and CC-BY-SA-4.0, compatible); GPL SUMO is confined to `ontology/reference` and never loaded. The `yggdrasil` package references BFO by verified IRI and copies nothing from SUMO (D46). No GPL content in loaded layers.
8. **Currency is enforced, not just requested.** D41 and D42: the cold-start entry point (`STATUS.md`) and the standing currency rule (`AGENTS.md`, auto-loaded, and restated in the `STATUS.md` and `DECISIONS.md` headers) mean a fresh session is told to read the state first and to keep this log and the status page current.
9. **The spike ratified the substrate without weakening an invariant.** D25, D32 and D38 moved to SETTLED on spike evidence (`spike/substrate/OUTCOME.md`), not by assumption. D45 records the one caveat (dense cyclic reachability) as a monitoring obligation. No invariant was weakened to pass; soundness held on every fuzzed operation.
10. **The seed was tested, not asserted, and grows without regressing.** D46 to D49: the ontology and Nornir run against a hand-labelled corpus and pass the four obligations (`ontology/OUTCOME.md`). Adding a second domain (D50) then the cross-domain priority principle (D52) moved coverage from 88.2 to 90.5 to 90.9 percent with no critical finding at any step: still zero downgrades, zero fail-safe breaches, sound reasoner, and the cross-domain state-staging fixtures caught. A downgrade, a fail-safe breach, an unsound derivation or a missed action-critical value would fail the suite; none occurred.
11. **Cross-domain priority is principled, not accidental.** D51 (the masking) is resolved by D52 (the priority principle): highest risk tier wins so nothing is masked down to inert, specificity settles same-tier contests so a scheduled task beats a bare action verb, and a genuine tie routes to `HIGH_RISK_UNRESOLVED` for human review rather than a silent pick. The harness proves both the resolved masking case (now an exact match) and a genuine tie (routed to review, still gated). No value reaches an inert type by masking, and no true tie is guessed.

**Known residual risk this log makes explicit:** the strongest live guarantee (structural separation, D02 to D09) is fully proven. The substrate mechanism (D25, D43 to D45) and the seed classifier and reasoner (D46 to D52) are demonstrated on small corpora, substrate-neutrally, across two domains with a principled cross-domain priority rule. The guarantee's *extent* still depends on ontology coverage growing beyond the seed, and on binding the proven algorithm to the live Memgraph store. The design is sound in mechanism, demonstrated on a two-domain seed, and unproven at full coverage. That is the honest state.
